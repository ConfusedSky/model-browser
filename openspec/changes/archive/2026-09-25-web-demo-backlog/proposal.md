changes and left a tail of items that have no change yet: some need a proposal, some
need a decision first, and three are corpus-side go-live gates that are not app work at
all. They were scattered across the notes' open items 1 and 3–7, its "Not drafted"
paragraph and its go-live gates, and the second session that held two of them closed on

ship, so nothing waits on a session that is gone.

It carries no code and no spec deltas. Each task line either produces a change of its
own or records a decision in the notes; the change archives when every line points at a
change, a recorded decision, or a done gate.
